## Rainbow Knight — Game Guide

**A 2D Zelda-style adventure in 13KB. Unicorn hero. Rainbow theme.**
Explore a big pixel overworld, gather resources, craft gear, slash gloom monsters, and recover the 7 rainbow crystals to restore the rainbow.

### Controls

**Desktop** — Move: WASD / Arrow keys (8 directions) · Attack: **Mouse click** (slash toward cursor) or Space / J / Z · Crafting: **E** / Tab · Retry: **R**
**Mobile** — D-pad (bottom-left) to move · **ATK** button (bottom-right) to slash · **CRAFT** button to open crafting

### Game Flow

1. **Goal** — Collect all **7 glowing rainbow crystals** to restore the rainbow. Progress shows as 7 diamonds in the top-right HUD; hearts top-left (5 max, +1 per Charm).
2. **Explore** — A 96×54 overworld of forests, lakes, rocks and rainbow flowers. Shallow water slows you to ~45% speed (splash particles; enemies slow too); deep water is impassable.
3. **Gather** — Chop trees ×2 → **Wood** · Mine rocks ×2 → **Ore** · Walk over flowers → **Petal** · Defeat monsters → **Essence** (50% drop). Monsters also drop hearts (30%).
4. **Craft (E)** — Potion (3 Petal + 1 Essence → +2 HP) · Blade (3 Ore + 2 Wood → +1 DMG, max 3) · Boots (2 Petal + 2 Essence → +15% speed) · Charm (2 Essence + 1 Ore → +1 max HP, max 8)
5. **Fight** — Blobs (purple, 2 HP) wander and chase; Spikers (dark, 3 HP) are faster and chase further. Each hit deals your Blade damage; touching an enemy costs 1 heart with brief invincibility.
6. **Win or lose** — Collect all 7 crystals → **"RAINBOW RESTORED!"** (with clear time). Hearts hit 0 → **"THE DARKNESS WINS."** Press R or tap to retry — the map regenerates identically.

**Tips** — Craft Blade early (one-shots blobs). Essence is the bottleneck, so farm monsters. Use shallow water for safe, slow escapes. Crystals sit in corners, edges and the center.
